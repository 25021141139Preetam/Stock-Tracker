Sentiment Analysis Dashboard

Product Category: Premium Mechanical Keyboards

📊 Sentiment Overview

💖 Top Liked Features

  • Aluminum / Metal case constructions 95%
  • Hot-swappable PCBs for easy modding 92%
  • Sound dampening (gasket mounts, foams) 88%
  • Open-source firmware (QMK/VIA) 75%

⚠️ Main Customer Complaints

  • "Gaming Tax" - overpriced mainstream brands High Volume
  • Software bloat and resource-heavy proprietary drivers High Volume
  • QC issues (double typing, dead sockets) over time Med Volume
  • Diminishing returns on ultra-premium ($500+) boards High Volume

✨ Wishlist Requests

  • More accessible premium low-profile options
  • Standardized parts for mainstream gaming boards
  • Better out-of-the-box tuning (no need to manually lube)
  • Wireless features with QMK/VIA support (like ZMK)

💰 Pricing Perception

Enthusiasts see the highest value in the $150 - $300 range (e.g., Keychron Q series, Monsgeek, barebones kits). Mainstream gaming boards costing $200+ are generally viewed as poor value due to non-standard parts and inferior acoustics.


Beyond $300, buyers acknowledge severe diminishing returns, shifting focus from "typing feel" to aesthetic exclusivity and limited-run group buys.
